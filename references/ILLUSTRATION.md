# Illustration research and asset provenance

The original painted instrument is saved at `dist/assets/concert-ukulele-gouache.png`. It was generated with the built-in image-generation tool, using a real Kala KA-C product photo as a construction reference. The product photograph itself is not served by the application.

Reference: [Kala KA-C, Satin Mahogany Concert Ukulele](https://kalabrand.com/products/ka-c). Reviewed 29 October 2026. The manufacturer's page gives a mahogany body and neck, cream binding, rosewood fingerboard, 18 frets, 14.8125-inch scale, 23.9375-inch overall length and 11.0625-inch body length. The original painting retains a concert-sized figure-eight body, four strings, four tuning machines, sound hole, bridge, saddle and nut. It is decorative artwork, not a product photograph or precision schematic.

The separate interactive teaching illustration in `dist/visuals.js` is authored as SVG. It has four string paths and eighteen fret positions computed with the equal-temperament formula `scale * (1 - 2^(-fret/12))`. Instrument parts, fret spacing, string continuity and silhouette were checked against the reference. The record-sleeve motifs are original geometric illustrations, not commercial album covers.

## Final generation prompt

Create an original, exceptionally beautiful editorial gouache and colored-pencil botanical-field-guide-style illustration of ONE real concert-size four-string ukulele, using the attached Kala KA-C front product image ONLY as a reference for the accurate instrument construction and proportions. Subject: warm honey-mahogany figure-eight body, smaller upper bout and rounder wider lower bout, distinct narrow waist, cream edge binding, dark rosewood fretboard, ROUND dark sound hole below the fretboard with a delicate rosette, dark tie bridge and ivory saddle, ivory nut, a short standard solid headstock with exactly FOUR silver geared tuners (two on either side) and exactly FOUR pale nylon strings running continuously from bridge over soundhole and fretboard to the headstock. Realistic concert proportions, overall length about 24 inches, body about 11 inches, 18 frets, 14.8-inch scale. Keep the visible fret spacing decreasing toward the body. Style: lovingly painted natural-history instrument illustration, realistic construction but visible delicate paint texture and wood grain, rich amber and toasted caramel wood, restrained highlights, elegant collectible music book aesthetic. Composition: full instrument entirely visible with generous transparent space around edges, upright with neck leaning 18 degrees toward the upper right, body centered lower, mild three-quarter depth but front face clearly legible. No hands, no person, no guitar with six strings, no additional instruments, no lettering, no logos or brand marks, no text, no musical symbols, no scene background, no paper rectangle. Genuine transparent background. Preserve distinct body, neck and headstock. This is a new painterly illustration, not a copy of the reference photo.
